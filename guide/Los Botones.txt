At the bottom of the screen, you will see eight buttons. They are numbered from left to right.

Button number one takes you to the next level.

Button number two, the button with the big X, takes you out of the program.

Button number three takes you back to the level selection page, where you can choose the level you want to study.

Button number four, the button with Jimmy Lucky’s picture, opens the guidelines page. This page gives you helpful ideas about how to use the program and how to make better progress with Spanish conversation.

Button number five opens a small contact screen. There you will find Jimmy Lucky’s email address, so you can send comments, questions, or suggestions.

Button number six, the money button, explains how you may donate to help support the continuation of Plática. Donations are welcome, but they are never required. The program is completely free.

Button number seven shows several of the characters used in the program. This button takes you to a screen where you can become familiar with the different character frames. As you continue using the program, you will naturally begin to recognize these frames and understand what each one represents.

Button number eight, the button on the far right, gives you a translation of what José García says. This translation gives you the general idea of what the page is about. The other characters on that page are speaking about the same basic idea, but from their own point of view.

You are encouraged to listen, observe, and make your best guess about what each person is saying. This is an important part of the method.

The purpose is not to translate every sentence word for word. The purpose is to help you begin thinking in Spanish. The translation from José García is only a clue. It gives you enough information to help you understand the rest of the page.

When people learn a language naturally, they do a lot of listening, guessing, and connecting ideas. This program follows that same approach.
