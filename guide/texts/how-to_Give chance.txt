1. Basic Instructions: How to Use the Page

On this page, you will see people or characters near the middle of the screen. You will also see different objects on both sides of the page.

The first thing to do is simple: click on things and listen. You can click on one of the people in the middle, and that person will speak to you. You can also click on one of the objects on the sides, and the object will say something about itself.

But the real purpose of this page is to let you build Spanish sentences by using the pictures. Try this: first click on one of the people or characters in the middle. Then, right after that, click on one of the objects on either side. Plática V2 will take those two parts and join them together into a complete Spanish sentence.

In other words, the person gives you the first part of the thought, and the object helps finish the thought. You are learning how Spanish ideas can be built piece by piece.

You do not have to understand everything immediately. Just start by using your mouse, or your finger if you are on a phone or tablet. Click a person. Then click an object. Listen to the sentence. Then try another combination.

You can also click on pictures just to hear what they say by themselves. And when you want to see a picture more clearly, you can right-click on it to make it larger.

So begin slowly. Click, listen, look, and try again. This page is made for practice. You are not expected to know everything before you start. You learn by using it.

2. Dale Una Oportunidad: Give It a Chance

Do not quit too soon.

At first, Plática V2 may feel a little different from the way you have studied before. That is normal. This program is not asking you to memorize a long list of words or study a grammar chart before you begin. It is asking you to look, listen, click, and let your mind begin to recognize the pattern.

So play with it a little.

Click on different people. Click on different objects. Listen to what happens. Try one combination, then try another one. You may not understand every sentence the first time you hear it, and that is perfectly all right. You are not failing. You are beginning.

Let the process start working.

Your mind is very good at finding patterns, especially when it hears something again and again in a meaningful situation. When you see the picture, hear the sound, and try different combinations, your brain begins to connect the ideas. Little by little, the sound starts to mean something. The sentence starts to feel familiar.

Trust that it will begin to make sense.

Give yourself a little time. Do not judge the whole program by the first few minutes. Stay with it long enough for the rhythm to begin working. Listen. Guess. Try again. Let the Spanish come to you piece by piece.

This is not a race. It is more like learning to enjoy a song. At first you only hear sounds. Then you begin to recognize parts. Then, after a while, it starts to stay with you.

So be patient with yourself. Play with it. Explore it. Let it work.

Dale una oportunidad.
